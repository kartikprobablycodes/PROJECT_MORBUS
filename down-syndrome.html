David's Unconditional Light

David, 24, works at a busy neighborhood café and acts in a local community theater troupe. While he processes complex abstract arithmetic at a slower pace and requires speech therapy, his ability to read emotional energy in a room is sharper than that of most corporate executives.

“When people look at me, some only see my eyes or the way I speak, and they speak to me like I’m a toddler. But when a customer walks in with tears in their eyes or hunched shoulders, I know immediately. I don't give them a lecture—I hand them their coffee, look straight into their heart, and ask: ‘Are you okay today?’ People cry because nobody else asked.”
